My Art at Gallery Rembrandt

I am proud to share that my paintings are currently on view at Gallery Rembrandt in Eindhoven.

A special step in my journey as an artist. My works are characterised by intuitive expression, organic forms and bold colour combinations. Each painting is born from feeling and develops in its own way.

Curious to see my work in person? You are warmly welcome to come and admire my paintings at Gallery Rembrandt in Eindhoven.
